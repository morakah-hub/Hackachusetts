# Robotics Lab v0.5.2: collision-checker correctness for box-box clearance

Branch `feature/v0.5.2-clearance-fix`, from v0.5.1 `2197959`. This milestone fixes the issue found in
v0.5.1 (brief §4.4) and validates the fix. It adds no planning method and no new capability. The
development log is [`v0.5.2-worklog.md`](v0.5.2-worklog.md). Earlier reports are unchanged.

## In one paragraph

MuJoCo 3.16's box-box collision routine does not report margin contacts reliably. Some pairs inside
the margin get no contact at all, and `contact.dist` can be off by several millimetres. The v0.5
planner's 20 mm clearance relied on those contacts, and the Panda's fingertip pads are boxes. We
audited every robot-environment pair type in our scenes against independently certified distances
(44,000 poses). Only box-box margin contacts fail (208 false "safe" answers, the closest 1.85 mm
from the obstacle). `mj_geomDistance` was correct on every audited pose. The collision checker now
uses `mj_geomDistance` for box-box robot/obstacle pairs, behind a conservative pre-filter, and keeps
MuJoCo's margin contacts for every other pair type, which the audit found correct. The change has
these effects:

- **Clearance.** Every planned path now keeps ≥ 20 mm at every checked sample and ≥ 19.998 mm
  densely in between. With the v0.5 checker, 3 of 113 benchmark plans came as close as 18.5 mm
  and fail re-validation by the corrected checker.
- **Success and accuracy.** Planning success rates are unchanged. All 90 executions succeed with
  0 contacts and the same final accuracy (≤ 0.1 mm).
- **Cost.** Median planning time rises by 6–46% in the obstacle scenes.

## 1. The problem, restated

The v0.5 checker sets `geom_margin = clearance` (20 mm) on environment geoms. It treats a
configuration as invalid when MuJoCo reports any robot-environment contact. Edges are sampled so
that no robot point moves more than `cartesian_step` = 10 mm between samples, which gives the
certificate "≥ 15 mm everywhere on a checked edge", but only if every sample's answer
"distance < 20 mm?" is correct. v0.5.1 showed that it is not correct for box-box pairs.

## 2. Audit of MuJoCo's distance queries (`tools/audit_clearance.py`)

**Setup.**
- **Pair models.** For each pair type in our scenes, an isolated two-geom model is built with the
  real Panda geometry (fingertip pad 17×8×17 mm, finger "teeth", finger/hand/link7 mesh hulls), the
  real obstacle, the checker's solver flags and a 20 mm margin.
- **Poses.** 4000 counted poses per pair type, each within 40 mm of the obstacle or overlapping it.
- **Overlap truth.** Decided by an LP over the robot geom's vertices.
- **Separation truth.** Certified by a duality gap below 1 µm. MuJoCo's witness points must lie in
  both geoms (upper bound), and a separating plane gives the lower bound.
- **No result from MuJoCo.** If `mj_geomDistance` finds nothing within its search radius, a
  separating plane must prove the pose is more than 40 mm away.

| pair (robot - environment) | types | separated < 20 mm | **contact: false safe** (closest) | contact: false alarm | contact.dist error (mm) | geomDistance: wrong answers | geomDistance error bound (mm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| fingertip pad - wall | box-box | 1191 | **40** (4.26 mm) | 7 | −4.07 .. +6.98 | 0 | 3e-9 |
| fingertip pad - table top | box-box | 1212 | **27** (4.66 mm) | 9 | −4.08 .. +5.96 | 0 | 2e-10 |
| finger tooth - wall | box-box | 1295 | **76** (1.85 mm) | 13 | −4.84 .. +3.18 | 0 | 1e-10 |
| fingertip pad - obstacle_1 | box-box | 1161 | **65** (2.09 mm) | 11 | −5.70 .. +6.62 | 0 | 1e-11 |
| finger mesh - wall | mesh-box | 1137 | 0 | 0 | 0.00 | 0 | 1e-10 |
| hand mesh - wall | mesh-box | 862 | 0 | 0 | 0.00 | 0 | 1e-10 |
| link7 mesh - table top | mesh-box | 926 | 0 | 0 | 0.00 | 0 | 5e-10 |
| fingertip pad - obstacle_2 | box-cylinder | 1208 | 0 | 0 | 0.00 .. +0.01 | 0 | 2e-5 |
| hand mesh - obstacle_2 | mesh-cylinder | 981 | 0 | 0 | 0.00 .. +0.02 | 0 | 5e-6 |
| fingertip pad - floor | box-plane | 1255 | 0 | 0 | 0.00 | 0 | 7e-15 |
| hand mesh - floor | mesh-plane | 362 | 0 | 0 | 0.00 | 0 | 1e-14 |

- **Penetration.** Detected by every method on every overlapping pose (17,665 poses).
- **Excluded poses.** 7 cylinder poses lay within ~3 µm of the curved surface, below what the
  64-gon prism bounds resolve, and were not counted.
- **Errors by method.** Only MuJoCo's box-box margin contacts are wrong: in both directions, and
  missed contacts as close as 1.85 mm. `mj_geomDistance` made no wrong decision for any pair
  type, and its error bound is ≤ 2e-5 mm.
- **Other probe findings.** A first probe also showed that `mj_geomDistance` ignores `geom_margin`
  and does not depend on its `distmax` (worklog §2).
- **What the audit covers.** It is evidence over random poses for the geom types in our scenes,
  not a proof for all poses. The tool is in the repository, so it can be re-run after a MuJoCo
  upgrade.

## 3. The fix (`planning/collision.py`)

- **Box-box pairs.** All other pairs still go through `mj_collision` with the clearance margin;
  the audit supports that. For **box-box robot/obstacle pairs**, MuJoCo's contacts are ignored and
  the answer comes from `mj_geomDistance`. In our scenes that means 10 finger boxes against the
  table top, the 4 legs, the wall, the ceiling or obstacle_1.
- **Pre-filter.** A pair is queried only if the robot box's center lies inside the obstacle's world
  AABB grown by clearance + the robot box's bounding radius. The AABB contains the obstacle and
  every point of the robot box lies within its bounding radius of the center. So a center outside
  that region on any axis proves distance ≥ clearance. The filter can make the checker do more
  work but can never accept a configuration that is too close.
- **Self-collision.** Unchanged: robot-robot pairs keep margin 0, i.e. penetration only.
- **Configuration.** `planning.exact_box_box: true` in `configs/planning_demo.yaml`,
  `benchmark_planning.yaml` and `robustness_wall.yaml` (also the default of `PlanningSettings` and
  `CollisionChecker`). `false` reproduces v0.5/v0.5.1 exactly: re-running the robustness planning
  in that mode gave all 30 v0.5.1 path lengths to the last digit. It exists for comparisons only.
- **Where it applies.** The planner, IK candidate validation, RRT-Connect edges, the shortcut,
  `path_valid` re-checks of old plans, and all clearance monitors (`min_distance`) use the
  corrected checker. The benchmark's independent re-validation always does, even when the planner
  under test runs in v0.5 mode.

## 4. Clearance between edge samples

**Derivation (unchanged from v0.5, now with exact per-sample answers).**
- **Lever-arm bound.** For any robot point p, |dp/ds| ≤ Σ_j R_j |Δq_j| along a straight joint
  edge. Here R_j bounds the distance from joint j's anchor to any collision geometry it moves;
  it does not depend on posture.
- **Sampling.** Samples are spaced so that this bound is ≤ `cartesian_step` = 10 mm per interval.
- **Consequence.** Every point on the edge is within 5 mm of its position at the nearer sample.
  Distance is 1-Lipschitz in position. So if every sample has distance ≥ 20 mm, every
  intermediate configuration has ≥ 15 mm.

**Assumptions under which this holds.**
1. The per-sample answer is exact: audited for our pair types, not proven.
2. The geometry is MuJoCo's: convex mesh hulls and primitives of the model, not the real robot.
3. The obstacles are static during the edge.
4. Self-collision is checked only at the samples (margin 0). A brief self-intersection between
   samples is not excluded.

So this is a certificate for robot-environment clearance in the model, conditional on (1). It is
**not** continuous collision checking and not a statement about the physical robot.

**Measured.**
- **Bound test.** `test_edge_samples_bound_how_far_any_robot_point_moves` follows every collision
  vertex of the Panda (box corners and mesh vertices) along random edges, with 8 sub-steps per
  interval. No vertex travels more than 10 mm between samples; the largest travel is >20% of
  the bound, so the bound is not vacuous.
- **Benchmark plans.** On 113 successful plans with the corrected checker, the exact minimum
  distance at the checked samples is ≥ 20.0 mm. Densely in between (every 1 mm of robot motion),
  the lowest value is **19.998 mm** and the largest drop between a path's sample minimum and its
  dense minimum is **0.07 mm**.
- **Robustness plans.** On the 30 robustness plans: samples ≥ 20.009 mm, dense ≥ 20.007 mm.
- **Interpretation.** The derived bound of 15 mm is very conservative for these paths. In practice
  the planner's paths stay within a few µm to 0.07 mm of the sample clearance.

## 5. Tests

`tests/test_clearance.py` (12 tests) and `tests/test_robustness.py`:

| case | what is checked |
|---|---|
| which pairs | exactly the 10 finger boxes × 6 box obstacles in the demo scene; nothing in v0.5 mode |
| configuration | `PlanningSettings`, `planning_demo.yaml` and the runner's planner carry `exact_box_box` |
| fingertip-wall near miss | Panda configuration, right pad 17.8 mm from the wall, every other pair ≥ 20.4 mm: v0.5 accepts, v0.5.2 rejects ("within clearance … finger-wall"), `min_distance` equals `mj_geomDistance` |
| fingertip just outside | pad 20.5 mm from the wall: v0.5.2 accepts, v0.5 rejected it (false alarm) |
| fingertip-wall collision | pad 3 mm inside the wall: both reject ("collision") |
| fingertip-table | pad 22 mm above the table: accepted; pad 2.8 mm inside the table top: rejected |
| isolated near misses | real pad size vs table top (2.13 mm) and wall (2.30 mm): MuJoCo generates no contact, the v0.5 checker accepts, the v0.5.2 checker rejects with the exact distance |
| ground truth near obstacles | 500 configurations jittered around wall plans: v0.5.2 agrees with exhaustive `mj_geomDistance` (+ self-collision) on every one; v0.5 does not |
| edge interpolation | lever-arm bound (above); planned paths: ≥ 20 mm at samples, ≥ 15 mm densely |
| former xfail | `test_checker_rejects_box_box_near_miss_inside_the_margin`: the v0.5.1 pose (1.25 mm) is rejected by the checker |
| MuJoCo pin | `test_mujoco_box_box_still_misses_this_margin_contact`: raw MuJoCo still misses it (alarms on a MuJoCo upgrade) |

**The v0.5.1 strict xfail.** It asserted that *raw MuJoCo* generates the contact, which remains
false in MuJoCo 3.16 whatever the checker does. It was resolved by moving the assertion to the
checker, where it passes, and keeping the MuJoCo behavior as an ordinary pinned test. The full
suite has **189 passed, 0 xfailed** (2 min 44 s).

**Exposure on the whole robot.**
- **Isolated pads.** A pad alone is missed as close as 1.85 mm.
- **Whole robot.** A random search with local descent found v0.5 accepting a pad at most
  **2.2 mm inside** the clearance (17.8 mm). Near the table it found no acceptance below 20 mm.
- **Why.** The finger meshes, which MuJoCo checks correctly, sit right behind the pads and usually
  trigger first.
- **Planner workload.** On 44,616 configurations the planner actually evaluated (wall scene, 10
  seeds), v0.5 made 91 false alarms and 0 false "safe" decisions.

So the practical effect was small (mm-level, mostly false alarms), but it did produce paths below
the stated clearance (§6.2).

## 6. Experiments: before (v0.5 checker) vs after (v0.5.2)

Everything was run sequentially on the same laptop (Windows 11, ~8 GB RAM, Python 3.13, MuJoCo
3.16.0), with nothing else running. "Before" = `exact_box_box: false`, run in the same session.
Execution "before" data are the published v0.5 / v0.5.1 runs; their contact-based clearance column
used the old monitor.

### 6.1 Collision-check cost (`tools/profile_collision.py`, `v0.5.2-profile.json`)

µs per configuration (best of 3 passes):

| workload | configs | mj_collision alone | v0.5 | **v0.5.2** | v0.5.2 without filter (all pairs) | exhaustive `mj_geomDistance` | geomDistance calls / config (filtered vs all) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| wall planner workload | 44,616 | 25 | 33 | **58** | 99 | 206 | 0.04 vs 59 |
| planning_demo, uniform random | 2000 | 22 | 25 | **37** | 84 | 160 | 0.00 vs 51 |
| obstacles, uniform random | 2000 | 20 | 22 | **35** | 81 | 239 | 0.00 vs 53 |
| planning_narrow, uniform random | 2000 | 52 | 66 | **67** | 78 | 200 | 0.00 vs 13 |

- **Correctness on these workloads.** v0.5.2 made 0 false safe and 0 false alarms in every
  workload; v0.5 made 0 / 91 on the planner workload.
- **Value of the filter.** It removes >99.9% of `mj_geomDistance` calls, and v0.5.2 is 1.2–2.3×
  cheaper than querying all pairs.
- **Where the overhead comes from.** It is the filter's numpy calls (up to ~25 µs per
  configuration), not `mj_geomDistance`, which runs a few hundred times per plan at most.

## 7. Regressions and their explanations

- **Planning is slower:** +6–46% median in the obstacle scenes, +40% in the robustness episodes.
  The cause is up to ~25 µs of numpy pre-filtering per configuration. Removing the filter is worse
  (all-pairs is 1.2–2.3× slower than v0.5.2). Pushing the filter into MuJoCo's C broadphase, e.g.
  through inflated finger-mesh margins, would recover most of it, but it complicates the
  correctness argument and self-collision semantics. Not done in this milestone.
- **Paths change:** 29/30 robustness episodes and the demo's second move. This is expected: the
  checker now accepts some configurations v0.5 falsely rejected (91 false alarms on the planner
  workload) and rejects a few it falsely accepted. Lengths are essentially unchanged on average.
- **Reported clearances look lower.** The old monitors showed e.g. 20.3 mm for paths whose exact
  clearance was 20.0 mm, and 16.2 mm for one that was really ~20 mm. All clearance columns are now
  exact for box-box pairs. Numbers in v0.5/v0.5.1 reports are not directly comparable to v0.5.2.
- No success rate, execution accuracy or contact count got worse.

## 8. Remaining limitations

- **Audited, not proven.** The correctness of `mj_geomDistance`, and of MuJoCo's margin contacts
  for non-box-box pairs, rests on 44,000 random poses over the pair types in these scenes. New
  geom types (capsules, ellipsoids, non-convex meshes) need the audit re-run and extended. The
  tool's pair list is easy to extend.
- **Only box-box robot/obstacle pairs are switched.** Box-box *self*-collision pairs (pad vs pad)
  still use MuJoCo's routine. That is fine at margin 0 (penetration is always detected) but is
  discrete only.
- **Discrete edge checking.** The 15 mm between-sample guarantee holds for MuJoCo's geometry,
  static obstacles, and exact per-sample answers. Measured drops are ≤ 0.07 mm, but nothing
  continuous is claimed. Self-collision between samples is not certified.
- **Simulation geometry only.** Convex hulls of the Panda meshes, exact known obstacles, no
  perception, no model error.
- **Timing.** Python wall-clock times on a loaded 8 GB laptop with ±25–30% run-to-run noise.
  Small samples (20 seeds per scenario, 5 per wall variant).
