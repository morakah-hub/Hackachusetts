## Stage 2-3: planner and collision checking (2026-10-19)

Probe of MuJoCo 3.16 collision queries on the `obstacles` scene (scratch script, not kept):

- `mj_kinematics + mj_collision` on a private model copy: 9 us per configuration with
  margin 0, 30 us with a 0.10 m margin on the environment geoms (more broadphase pairs).
- With `geom_margin` set on environment geoms, `contact.dist` equals `mj_geomDistance`
  to 1e-5 m for the mesh-box pairs (native CCD gives true distances, not only
  penetration). So a clearance check is just MuJoCo's contact margin on a model copy;
  robot-robot pairs keep margin 0.
- Lever-arm bounds R_j (anchor of joint j to farthest collision geom it moves):
  [1.149, 1.149, 0.833, 0.751, 0.358, 0.358, 0.270] m. Used for (a) edge sampling
  density (no robot point moves > 10 mm between samples) and (b) the default RRT metric
  weights (R_j / R_1, floored at 0.2): [1, 1, 0.725, 0.653, 0.311, 0.311, 0.235].

Design decisions:

- Clearance 20 mm + Cartesian step 10 mm: any configuration on a checked edge is
  >= 15 mm from the environment (distance is 1-Lipschitz; every point is within half a
  step of a sample). Self-collision has margin 0 and is discrete only.
- RRT-Connect with preallocated node arrays and vectorized nearest neighbour; edge checks
  coarse-to-fine (midpoint first) for early rejection.
- Three budgets with distinct statuses: time (`timeout`), nodes (`node_budget`),
  iterations (`no_path`). Direct edge tried first and its validity always recorded.
- Planner never touches live MjData; only a read of finger qpos at construction.

First results, `obstacles` scene, the v0.3 test pair (0.68, 0.42, 0.52) -> (0.70, 0.18, 0.52):

- The old IK solution for (0.68, 0.42, 0.52) is only 17 mm from `obstacles` (link7): rejected
  by the 20 mm clearance as a start. Planning home -> a -> b instead: a needs RRT (direct
  edge from home blocked), b: 6/6 seeds succeed, 0.12-0.18 s including IK, 7-13 nodes,
  raw 2.3-4.6 rad -> shortcut 1.2-2.6 rad.

## Stage 3b: a false negative in the clearance check (2026-10-19)

- New test `test_planned_reference_is_continuous_feasible_and_collision_free` samples the
  executed demo reference and measures the true distance to the environment. It found a
  configuration 8.8 mm from the wall (right finger), below the 15 mm the sampling argument
  promises, on an edge whose 92 samples had all been accepted.
- Cause: MuJoCo's **midphase** (per-body BVH culling, active for bodies with several
  geoms such as the fingers) does not account for the other geom's margin. At the sample
  the true finger-wall distance was 8.5 mm (`mj_geomDistance`) under a 20 mm margin, yet
  `mj_collision` returned no contact; with `mjDSBL_MIDPHASE` it returned the pair
  (0.0075-0.0174 m). Setting the margin on the finger geoms too did not help.
- Ground truth over 3000 random configurations per scene (exhaustive `mj_geomDistance` over
  all collidable robot/environment pairs, excluded base-table pair removed):
  midphase on: 14 of 91 (planning_demo) and 16 of 74 (obstacles) near-miss configurations
  missed (~20%); midphase off: 0 false negatives, 0 false positives, 24-26 us per check.
- Fix: the checker's private model copy disables the midphase. Plant unchanged (with
  margin 0 the plant only needs penetrations, where the midphase is fine).
  `test_checker_matches_exhaustive_distance_ground_truth` guards it.
- Free direct moves now keep `move_time` when planning is enabled, so a planned task whose
  moves are all free is identical to the v0.4 task (test).
- Full suite: 158 passed.

## Stage 5b: shortcut fixes (2026-10-19)

- Measured shortcut iterations on 12 raw wall paths (mean raw 3.65 rad, 9.1 vertices).
  Length *grew* with more iterations (100: 1.71 rad, 800: 2.37). Two causes:
  1. Shortcuts were accepted by the weighted metric (wrist weighted 0.2-0.3) but the
     unweighted length is reported; trading shoulder for wrist motion lowered one and raised
     the other. Now the shortcut minimizes the reported unweighted joint-space length.
  2. Long runs failed the final re-validation and fell back to the raw path. New vertices on a
     validated edge are only certified to clearance - step/2 (15 mm), while re-validation
     requires 20 mm at its own, new sample positions; the same for the shortened old edges.
     Shortcuts now check the new vertices and both partial edges as `path_valid` will.
  After: 0 iterations (pruning only) 1.97 rad / 3.4 vertices, 50: 1.47 / 4.6, 100: 1.45 / 4.7,
  400: 1.40 / 5.8, 800: 1.40 / 5.8; no fallbacks. Kept 100 (shorter paths cost more stops).
- `planning/__init__.py` re-exported the `shortcut` function, shadowing the submodule
  (`import planning.shortcut as S` returned the function). Removed the re-export.
